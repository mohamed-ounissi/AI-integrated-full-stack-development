# FAQ: Customer has a flat tire and needs roadside assistance

Roadside drivers carry equipment to install the vehicle's own spare tire on-site, if the vehicle has one. This is a standard part of the roadside-assistance visit and doesn't require towing.

If the vehicle has no spare (common on many newer models that ship with only a repair kit or run-flat tires) or the spare is also flat, the vehicle needs to be towed to the nearest AutoCare location instead.

Ask the customer to check their trunk or owner's manual for a spare before dispatch is finalized, since this determines whether the visit is a quick roadside fix or a tow, which affects both response time and vehicle downtime expectations set with the customer.
