# FAQ: Customer reports a grinding noise when braking

A high-pitched squeal when braking is usually just the wear indicator tab doing its job — it's designed to make noise when pads are getting low, and isn't urgent on its own.

A grinding or growling noise is more serious: it usually means the pads are fully worn through and metal is contacting metal against the rotor, which can mean the rotors now need replacing too, not just the pads.

Advise customers reporting grinding (not squealing) to book an appointment promptly — continued driving on a grinding brake increases both the repair cost and the safety risk. This should be treated with similar urgency to a flashing check engine light.
