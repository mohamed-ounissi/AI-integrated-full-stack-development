# FAQ: Customer says the AC is blowing warm air

The most common cause by far is low refrigerant due to a slow leak — AC systems don't consume refrigerant in normal use, so if the level is low, it's leaking somewhere.

Other causes: a failing compressor (often noisy or cycling on/off rapidly), a blocked cabin air filter reducing airflow, or a faulty blend door actuator that's stuck mixing in warm air.

Ask whether the air is warm all the time or only sometimes — intermittent warm air points toward the blend door actuator or a compressor that's cycling incorrectly, while constant warm air points toward low refrigerant or a fully failed compressor.
