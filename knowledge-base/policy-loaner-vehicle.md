# Policy: Loaner vehicle eligibility

A loaner vehicle is available free of charge for any service expected to take longer than 4 hours or require an overnight stay, subject to availability at that location.

Customers must have a valid driver's license and proof of insurance on file to receive a loaner. There is no mileage limit on loaners, but they must be returned with a full tank of fuel or a refueling fee applies.

Loaners are not available for roadside-assistance-only visits (e.g. a tow with no follow-up repair) — only for in-shop service exceeding the 4-hour threshold.
