# FAQ: How often should tires be rotated?

AutoCare recommends tire rotation every 8,000-12,000 km, or at every other oil change for customers on the standard interval — an easy way to remember it without tracking a separate schedule.

Rotation evens out wear between front and rear tires, since front tires (on front-wheel-drive vehicles especially) wear faster due to handling steering and most of the braking load. Skipping rotations shortens overall tire life and can cause uneven wear that affects handling.

This is a good add-on to mention during any oil change visit, especially for customers who haven't had one in the last two visits — check the service history for the last rotation date before assuming it's due.
