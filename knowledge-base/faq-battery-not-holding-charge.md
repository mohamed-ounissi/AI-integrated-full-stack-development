# FAQ: Customer says their battery isn't holding charge overnight

This is usually one of three things: a battery reaching end of life (see the battery replacement warranty policy for coverage), a parasitic draw from something staying powered on overnight (interior light, aftermarket accessory, faulty relay), or a failing alternator that isn't fully recharging the battery while driving.

Ask the customer how old the battery is and whether they've noticed dimming lights or slow cranking during the day, not just in the morning — that points toward the alternator rather than the battery itself. A load test and a parasitic draw test both take under 20 minutes and should be done before quoting a battery replacement.
