# Policy: Roadside assistance response time targets

During business hours (8am-7pm local time), the target response window for roadside assistance is 20-40 minutes from the time a request is confirmed, depending on driver availability and distance.

If a driver hasn't arrived within 50 minutes during business hours, the customer is eligible for a service credit on their next visit, applied automatically once the ticket is marked resolved late.

Response time targets are best-effort, not guaranteed — weather, traffic, and simultaneous high-volume requests (e.g. after a storm) can extend these windows, and agents should communicate that honestly rather than promising an exact time they can't guarantee.
