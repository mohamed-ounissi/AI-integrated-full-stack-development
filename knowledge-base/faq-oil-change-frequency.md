# FAQ: How often should a customer get an oil change?

For most modern vehicles using synthetic oil, AutoCare recommends every 8,000-10,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first. This is longer than the older "every 5,000 km" rule of thumb, which applied to conventional oil.

Vehicles under severe driving conditions — frequent short trips, towing, extreme heat or cold, or heavy stop-and-go traffic — should follow the shorter 6-month interval regardless of mileage, since oil degrades faster under those conditions even if the odometer hasn't moved much.

Always check the specific vehicle's manufacturer recommendation when available; this is a general guideline, not a substitute for the owner's manual interval.
