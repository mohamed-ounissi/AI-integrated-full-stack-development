# Policy: Battery replacement warranty

Batteries installed by AutoCare carry a 24-month, unlimited-mileage warranty covering full replacement if the battery fails a load test within that period.

Between 24 and 36 months, the battery is covered under a prorated warranty: the customer pays a percentage of the current retail price based on how many months into the 12-month prorated window they are. After 36 months, no warranty coverage applies.

To honor a warranty claim, the battery must have been installed by AutoCare (check the service history for a "battery replacement" line item) and must fail an in-shop load test — a customer's own claim that "it seems weak" isn't sufficient on its own without the test result.
