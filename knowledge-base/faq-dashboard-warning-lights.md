# FAQ: How urgent are different dashboard warning lights?

Red warning lights mean stop driving as soon as it's safe to do so — this includes oil pressure, battery/charging system, and brake system warnings. These indicate a problem that can cause serious damage or a safety risk within minutes of continued driving.

Yellow/amber warning lights (check engine, low coolant, tire pressure) mean get it checked soon, but the vehicle is generally safe to drive a limited distance in the meantime — these are the majority of roadside and diagnostic calls.

When in doubt about a light the customer describes but you don't recognize, ask for the exact icon or wording rather than guessing — treating an amber warning as an emergency (or vice versa) either alarms the customer unnecessarily or under-responds to a real risk.
