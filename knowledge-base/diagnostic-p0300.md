# Diagnostic code P0300 — Random/Multiple Cylinder Misfire Detected

P0300 means the engine control unit detected misfires across multiple cylinders, not isolated to just one (a single-cylinder misfire would show as P0301, P0302, and so on instead).

Common causes: worn spark plugs or ignition coils, a vacuum leak affecting multiple cylinders, low fuel pressure, or contaminated fuel.

Recommended first step: this code is more urgent than most — a sustained misfire can damage the catalytic converter within minutes of driving. Advise the customer to minimize driving until it's checked. Start by checking spark plug condition and ignition coil health, since those are the most common cause and the fastest to inspect.
