# Policy: Towing requests after business hours

AutoCare's roadside assistance line is staffed 24/7, but towing dispatch outside of business hours (before 8am or after 7pm local time) follows a different process than daytime requests.

After-hours requests are routed to our partner towing network rather than in-house drivers. Expect a slightly longer response window: typically 45-75 minutes after-hours, versus 20-40 minutes during business hours. Customers should be told this difference up front when they call after hours, so expectations are set correctly.

Emergency situations (blocked traffic lane, unsafe location, medical concern) are always escalated immediately regardless of time of day, and should never wait for the standard dispatch queue.
