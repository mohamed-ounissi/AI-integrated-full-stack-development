# FAQ: What should I tell a customer whose check engine light is on?

A steady check engine light means a fault was detected but it's not typically urgent — the vehicle is generally safe to drive to an appointment. A **flashing** check engine light is different and means an active misfire that can damage the catalytic converter; the customer should stop driving as soon as safely possible.

Advise customers to avoid heavy acceleration or towing until the vehicle is checked. Booking a diagnostic appointment (not a full repair appointment) is the right first step, since the specific stored code determines what repair is actually needed.
