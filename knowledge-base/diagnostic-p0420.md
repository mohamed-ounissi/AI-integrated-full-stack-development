# Diagnostic code P0420 — Catalyst System Efficiency Below Threshold (Bank 1)

P0420 means the engine control unit sees the catalytic converter converting exhaust gases less efficiently than expected. It compares the readings from the oxygen sensors before and after the catalytic converter; when they look too similar, the converter isn't doing its job.

Common causes, roughly in order of likelihood: a failing catalytic converter, a faulty or aging oxygen sensor (especially the downstream one), an exhaust leak near the manifold or converter, or an engine misfire that damages the converter over time.

Recommended first step: check for other stored codes first, especially misfire codes (P0300-series). Fixing a misfire before replacing the converter can save a customer from replacing a part that failed as a side effect of a different problem. If no misfire codes are present, test both oxygen sensors before assuming the converter itself has failed.
