# FAQ: Customer says the engine won't start

First distinguish between two very different problems: does the engine crank (turn over, making a cranking sound) but not start, or does it not crank at all (silence, or just a click)?

No crank at all, or a single click, almost always points to a dead or failing battery, or a bad starter in rarer cases. This is the most common roadside-assistance call and is usually resolved on-site with a jump-start, followed by a recommendation to test the battery.

Cranks but doesn't start points to a fuel delivery or ignition problem instead (empty tank, failed fuel pump, bad ignition coil) and typically can't be resolved roadside — it needs a tow to a shop for diagnosis.
