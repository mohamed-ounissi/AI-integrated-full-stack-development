# FAQ: Customer's dashboard shows a low coolant warning

A low coolant warning means the coolant reservoir level sensor is reading below the minimum mark — it's a level warning, not necessarily an overheating warning (that would show as a separate temperature gauge warning).

Advise the customer it's safe to drive a short distance to a service location, but not to ignore it for long — running low on coolant for an extended period can eventually lead to overheating and, in the worst case, engine damage.

Common causes: a slow coolant leak (check under the vehicle for a puddle, often sweet-smelling), a failing radiator cap not holding pressure correctly, or in rare cases a blown head gasket if there is also white exhaust smoke or a rising temperature gauge.
