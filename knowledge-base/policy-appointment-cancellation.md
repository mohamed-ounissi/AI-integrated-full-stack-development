# Policy: Service appointment cancellation

Customers can cancel or reschedule a service appointment free of charge up to 4 hours before the scheduled time.

Cancellations within 4 hours of the appointment, or no-shows, incur a $25 late-cancellation fee, waived once per customer per rolling 12-month period as a courtesy.

Diagnostic and roadside-assistance appointments are exempt from this fee entirely, since those are typically booked in response to an urgent issue and short-notice changes are expected.
